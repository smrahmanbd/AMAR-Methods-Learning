Remember

The crash investigator’s road trip

Memory champions remember lists by placing strange pictures along a route they know. Here is a route with seven stops, one per idea. The stranger the picture, the better it sticks.

Drive the route

Press Next stop, or click any stop. Read the scene, close your eyes for five seconds and picture it. Add sound and movement.

Why it works: the route gives the order, the picture gives the meaning, and the number hidden in each scene (100 houses, 18 opinions) gives you a worked example to rebuild the idea from.

Flip cards

Say the answer aloud before you turn the card. Saying it is what builds the memory; reading the back does not.

Self-test: put the route in order

Click the stops in the order you meet them on the trip.

A second place: the Logit Hotel

The road trip holds the seven ideas. The models live in a hotel with six floors. Each floor adds one thing to the floor below, just as each model adds one thing to the model before.

  1. Ground floor · the two-way door (binary logit). The hotel entrance is a revolving door with only two exits, IN and OUT. A doorman reads your score and leans on the door: the higher the score, the harder he pushes you towards IN, but he can never push with 100% certainty.
  2. Floor 1 · the balloon buffet (multinomial logit). Every dish on the buffet has a balloon tied to it. The tastier the dish (the higher its score), the bigger its balloon. Your chance of picking a dish is its balloon’s share of all the air in the room.
  3. Floor 2 · the twin rooms (nested logit). Twins share one room and one key. When one twin checks out, the other twin inherits nearly all of the luggage. The guest in the single room across the corridor gets almost nothing.
  4. Floor 3 · the thermometer staircase (ordered logit and probit). A giant thermometer runs up the stairwell with two gates across it. One blob of mercury rises with the score. Below the first gate: no injury. Between the gates: injury. Above the second gate: fatal.
  5. Floor 4 · the costume party (mixed (random parameters) logit). The costume shop from the road trip has sent everyone here. Each guest has a different taste, so each has a different chance of dancing. The hotel manager reports only the average of all those chances.
  6. Floor 5 · the masked ball (latent class logit). Two secret clubs are at a masked ball. You cannot tell who belongs to which club. You only know that 60% wear one kind of ring under their glove, and each club dances to its own rules.
