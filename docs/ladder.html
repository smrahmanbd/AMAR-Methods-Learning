The ladder in four steps

All four models give the probability of each injury outcome. They differ only in what a coefficient (the effect of a variable) is allowed to do. Here the coefficient is the effect of not wearing a seat belt on the severe-injury score.

Rung 1 · fixed

MNL: multinomial logit

Pn(i) = eβiXin ÷ Σ eβIXIn

One coefficient for everyone. Simple and fast, but assumes all crashes respond the same way, and has the IIA property.

Rung 2 · spread

RPL: random parameters logit

βn = β + σνn

The coefficient varies across crashes (unobserved heterogeneity). Probabilities become integrals, estimated by simulated maximum likelihood.

Rung 3 · shift

RPLHM: heterogeneity in means

βn = β + ΘZn + σνn

The centre of the distribution moves with observed variables Z, such as a young driver.

Rung 4 · stretch

RPLHMV: heterogeneity in means and variances

βn = β + ΘZn + σeωWnνn

The width of the distribution also changes with observed variables W, such as night-time.
